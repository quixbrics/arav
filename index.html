TRANSCRIBE WITH WHISPER

Runs OpenAI's Whisper speech recognition in your browser (nothing is uploaded) and times every word. It uses the vocal stem if you've loaded one, which is much more accurate on songs. The model downloads once (~80–150 MB).

Or type or paste lyrics by hand: one line per phrase, starting with its time in seconds. Words in a line are spread across it. Tip: play the track and press ⌘/Ctrl+Enter in the editor to drop in the current time.
